Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Our Commitment

AgroTech is committed to fair and transparent transactions between farmers and buyers. We take quality seriously. If you are not satisfied with your order, here's what you can do.

2. When You Can Request a Refund

  • The produce received is significantly different from what was listed.
  • The order was not delivered within the expected timeframe.
  • The crop quality is poor, damaged, or not fresh.
  • You received the wrong item or wrong quantity.

3. How to Raise a Refund

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery. You can raise a dispute directly from your order tracking page. Attach clear photos of the received produce.

4. Resolution Timeline

Our team will review your complaint within 3–5 working days. If the refund is approved, it will be credited to your original payment method within 7 working days.

5. What is Not Covered

  • Refund requests made after 48 hours of delivery.
  • Cases where the buyer provided incorrect delivery address.
  • Minor variations in size, color, or shape of natural produce.
  • If you change your mind after placing an order (non-perishable exceptions apply).

6. Farmer Responsibility

Farmers are expected to provide accurate product descriptions and maintain quality. Repeated complaints against a farmer may result in account suspension.

7. Contact Support

For any disputes or queries, email us at support@agrotech.in or write to us through your profile page.
